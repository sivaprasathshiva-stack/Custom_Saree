## NILA-REF-001 — nila-master-primary.png
**Purpose:** Canonical identity anchor for every future Nila generation or campaign use.
**When to use:** Always, as the primary reference wherever a generation system accepts a single reference image. Supply alongside supporting references when a system accepts multiple.
**What it preserves:** The full-face, front-leaning identity — this is the image every other Nila image should be checked against for consistency.
**Primary or supporting:** Primary.

## NILA-REF-002 — nila-front-portrait.png
**Purpose:** Clean, direct front-facing facial reference.
**When to use:** Any generation requiring precise frontal facial geometry (packaging, ID-style brand assets, front-facing hero crops).
**What it preserves:** Frontal symmetry, eye spacing, nose/mouth alignment as seen head-on.
**Primary or supporting:** Supporting.

## NILA-REF-003 — nila-3q-left.png
**Purpose:** Three-quarter left facial angle.
**When to use:** Editorial compositions where Nila is angled toward the left of frame; multi-angle generation systems.
**What it preserves:** Left-side facial contour and how the identity reads at an angle.
**Primary or supporting:** Supporting.

## NILA-REF-004 — nila-3q-right.png
**Purpose:** Three-quarter right facial angle.
**When to use:** Mirror case of NILA-REF-003, for right-angled compositions.
**What it preserves:** Right-side facial contour.
**Primary or supporting:** Supporting.

## NILA-REF-005 — nila-profile.png
**Purpose:** Full side-profile reference.
**When to use:** Any generation or layout requiring a true profile (silhouette-driven editorial, side-angle campaign shots).
**What it preserves:** Nose bridge, chin line and ear position in profile — the angle most prone to identity drift if omitted.
**Primary or supporting:** Supporting.

## NILA-REF-006 — nila-full-body.png
**Purpose:** Full-body proportion and drape reference.
**When to use:** Any full-body campaign, lookbook, or product-on-body generation.
**What it preserves:** Overall body proportions and how a saree drapes on Nila specifically, so future full-body generations stay proportionate to this reference rather than an arbitrary default.
**Primary or supporting:** Supporting (but the only proportion reference — treat as required, not optional, for any full-body work).

## NILA-REF-007 — nila-neutral-expression.png
**Purpose:** Neutral/composed expression reference.
**When to use:** Editorial or documentation contexts calling for a composed, non-smiling register (e.g. Saree Passport imagery, technical/process content).
**What it preserves:** The baseline expression range, distinct from the warmer smiling references.
**Primary or supporting:** Supporting.

## NILA-REF-008 — nila-natural-smile.png
**Purpose:** Natural smiling expression reference.
**When to use:** Warmer campaign or homepage contexts where an approachable expression suits the brand mood.
**What it preserves:** How Nila's smile should look — natural, not exaggerated, consistent with the master primary reference.
**Primary or supporting:** Supporting.

## NILA-REF-009 — nila-hair-variation.png
**Purpose:** Documents an alternate hair styling (hair-up vs. hair-down) for the same identity.
**When to use:** Any generation calling for a different hairstyle than the loose-hair default seen in most references.
**What it preserves:** Confirms hair-up styling is within the approved variation range without changing hair colour/texture identity.
**Primary or supporting:** Supporting.
