## Visual Identity

Descriptions below are limited to what is actually observable in the nine reference images (`nila-master-primary.png` and `nila-full-body.png` were reviewed directly to ground this document; the remaining seven were catalogued but not individually re-described here). No measurement, HEX value, age, height or weight is stated anywhere in this document — none of those are determinable from a photograph, and the source brief explicitly prohibits inventing them.

- **Face** — Oval-leaning face shape with softly defined cheekbones.
- **Eyes** — Dark brown, warm in expression, with naturally shaped brows.
- **Hair** — Long, dark brown to black, with a soft natural wave; styled loose in the reference set, with one reference (`nila-hair-variation.png`) documenting an up/down styling variation.
- **Skin appearance** — Warm medium skin tone, photographed under natural/warm interior lighting; no visible heavy retouching or plastic/beauty-filter texture in the reviewed images.
- **Facial structure** — Consistent across the two reviewed references: same jaw, brow and nose proportions, same general structure.
- **Expression** — Warm, approachable, quietly confident — a soft closed or near-closed smile rather than a broad grin, in both reviewed images.
- **Overall presence** — Composed, editorial, at ease in traditional dress rather than performing for the camera.
- **Styling** — Seen in a cream/ivory silk saree with a gold woven border in both reviewed references, gold jhumka-style earrings, a small bindi, and delicate gold bangles. This is the observed *reference-set* styling, not a fixed permanent wardrobe — see Allowed Variation below.
- **Fashion language** — Contemporary heritage: traditional silhouette and jewellery vocabulary, photographed in a restrained, editorial (not ornamental/festive) register.

## Identity Invariants

The following must remain consistent unless changed through an intentional, documented brand update (see `NILA_CHANGELOG.md`):

- Recognizable facial identity across the reference set
- Apparent adult age range consistent with the references (no explicit number given)
- Overall facial structure (jaw, brow, cheekbone proportions)
- Eye shape and eye colour (dark brown)
- Nose structure
- Lip shape
- Hair colour (dark brown/black) and general texture (soft natural wave)
- General skin tone/appearance
- Overall body proportions as shown in `nila-full-body.png`
- Overall visual presence and expression register (warm, composed, understated)

## Allowed Variation

Nila may change:
- Saree, blouse, border, pallu, zari, colour
- Jewellery
- Hairstyle (within the hair-variation reference already established)
- Pose
- Location
- Lighting
- Expression
- Camera angle
- Campaign styling

...provided her identity remains recognizably the same person against the invariants above.

## Prohibited Identity Drift

Avoid:
- Different ethnicity or fundamentally different appearance
- Significantly different facial structure
- Substantially different apparent age
- Different eye shape or colour
- Dramatically different nose or lips
- Dramatically different body proportions
- Resemblance to any real, identifiable celebrity or public figure
- Random model substitution between campaigns
