- Quiet luxury
- Modern Indian heritage
- Sophisticated
- Intelligent

- Confident

- International

## Photography Style

Based on the existing reference set and VELVOREA's established "quiet luxury × technical precision" direction (see `.claude/skills/silk-design/SKILL.md`):

**Prefer:**
- Professional editorial photography
- Realistic skin and hair rendering
- Physically accurate silk — visible weave, real drape and fold behaviour
- Natural fabric folds, not stylised/impossible drape
- Realistic, motivated lighting (the reference set uses warm, natural-feeling interior/courtyard light)
- Restrained colour grading
- Authentic photographic characteristics — grain, depth of field, real optics

**Avoid:**
- Plastic/over-smoothed skin
- Beauty-filter appearance
- Excessive HDR
- Oversaturation
- CGI or 3D-render appearance
- Fantasy lighting
- Excessive glamour styling
- Generic stock-photography posing

## Fashion Language

Contemporary heritage: traditional saree silhouette and jewellery vocabulary (bindi, jhumka earrings, bangles), styled with editorial restraint rather than maximalist festive styling. The reference set shows Nila in a cream/ivory silk saree with a gold woven border — this establishes the *tonal* direction (warm neutrals, gold-on-ivory) as a default, not a fixed permanent wardrobe. Future campaigns are expected to show her in other VELVOREA designs, per the product's actual palette range.

## Makeup

Minimal, refined editorial makeup — consistent with what's visible in the reference set: natural base, soft eye definition, a muted lip tone. Not a heavy bridal or stage-makeup look by default.

## Jewellery

Understated luxury — gold jhumka-style earrings, a small bindi, delicate bangles in the reference set. Do not default to excessive/maximal bridal jewellery styling unless a specific bridal campaign calls for it (see `/bridal` on the website for the product-side bridal direction this would tie into).

## Hair

Long, dark, softly waved hair worn loose is the default seen across most references. `nila-hair-variation.png` documents an approved up-style variation. Hair colour and texture are identity invariants (see `NILA_MASTER_IDENTITY.md`); styling (up/down, parted, accessorised) is an allowed variation.

## Backgrounds

**Preferred:**
- Contemporary architecture with heritage character (the reference set uses a warm stone/wood interior with temple-adjacent detailing — columns, brass lamps)
- Textile studios
- Minimal interiors
- Refined Indian environments
- Neutral editorial environments (plain backdrops for portrait/product-focused work)
- Authentic craft environments — only when the photography is genuinely documentary (see `NILA_GENERATION_RULES.md`, Rule 9 — Nila must never stand in for a real documentary subject)
