# NILA Usage Guidelines

## Website
Approved for:
- Homepage
- Campaign pages
- Editorial content
- Lookbooks
- Studio education/onboarding content
- Brand storytelling

Not yet integrated into any live page — see `README.md` for current integration status. Adding Nila to a page should be a deliberate decision per page, not automatic.

## Social Media
Approved for:
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- Pinterest
- Campaign content on other platforms as the brand expands

## Product Photography
Use only when the generated saree/design she's shown wearing accurately represents the actual VELVOREA product being marketed (see `NILA_GENERATION_RULES.md`, Rule 10). Never use a Nila image where the product shown doesn't match what's actually being sold.

## Do Not Use
Do not use Nila to falsely represent:
- A real customer
- A real artisan
- A real employee
- A real factory worker
- A real event attendee
- A real testimonial

...unless the image is explicitly and clearly presented as fictional/editorial AI content. Nila is a brand device, not a stand-in for real people VELVOREA has not actually photographed. This is a hard rule, not a style preference — it protects both the brand's credibility and the people (real weavers, real customers) whose actual stories the product requirements elsewhere insist must never be fabricated.
